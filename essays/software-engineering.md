## I. Introduction
Artificial intelligence has become a rather prominent topic in recent years with the many great advancements that it has had. What was once just a pipe dream bound to the realm of science fiction has become something so close to becoming reality as we see articial intelligence become much smarter and capable of producing rather possible products. It has, however, become quite controversial in some sectors, such as in creative spaces where it is often used to copy artstyles and mass produce art, bringing concerns for how creative jobs will evolve in the future. For our purposes, however, we will be focusing on one of the other controversial sectors for artificial intelligence, education. While artificial intelligence can be a rather useful tool as a repository of information, it is also commonly abused by students to do their work for them, making the point of education rather moot in many cases. It has become a boogieman that threatens the credibility of each student's work as not a product of their own efforts and learning, making it hard for teachers to assess a student's capabilities. For my field of study, computer science, it is often used to produce code for assignments. While I personally have not made use of AI in my work, it certainly provides a tempting shortcut for assignments, so that people do not have to memorize certain aspects of langauges and quickly produce a seemingly functional program. Software engineering could possibly take much less time as artificial intelligence is able to implement frameworks and general code for the coder. The problem comes from the total reliance on artificial intelligence, as it is still in a rather primitive state that can be prone to errors that humans might have been able to catch. Computers, after all, are still far off from the capabilities of the human brain in information processing.
