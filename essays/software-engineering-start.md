Computer science is a wide and varied field focused on computer related concepts including algorithms, data, and a whole lot of coding. One of the most well known aspects of computer might be the field of software engineering. Software engineering, as its name implies, focuses on producing functional and secure software and programs that people will then be able to use. It could be considered the practical application of the various concepts of computer science in order to produce something useful. It is an intriguing field to be sure, that I am intrigued to further explore and further build upon the skills that I have nurtured through my own computer science experiences.

## Software Engineering

In my own experiences learning various computer science topics and acclimating myself to different programming languages, I have never really built my own program outside of various exercises in classes. Theoretically I should be able to create at least a rudimentary program, but in practice I don't have that much to show for it. That is why I am excited to dip my toes into software engineering and trying my best to develop some programs and apply myself. The focus is no longer on concepts and theory, but on a strict discipline to create the best product based upon those concepts and theories. Eventually, as I go off into the real world and hopefully continue down this path of computer science, than I am going to be developing a lot of software, so going down this path and learning more about it will only proceed to help light my way forward.

Experiencing software engineering is not just about coding; however, as it is also an exercise in discipline and various skills in order to develop the best product in a myriad of circumstances. As with most things, software engineering is never as simple as building a single program. There also exists quality assurance and error checking as with any product to be developed, which are skills that have to be learned and refined with experience trying over and over again as we encounter scenarios we could never even dream of. There is also the aspect of group work, which is a truth with almost any field, as practically any job will require some form of human interaction and cooperation. The course I take is definitely going to include group work, so it will hopefully be a nice experience to learn and further develop my own ability to work with other people. Every experience is an opportunity to learn so many things, beyond what you came for.

## Conclusion

I am excited to go on learning software engineering, and I hope to further develop my own self. It will be an interesting experience for sure as I try to apply myself towards this experience alongside my peers. It will be something to look back upon fondly for sure, with all that I will learn and do.
